Grand City Tour

A longer street kart run covering more of central Osaka. Built for riders who want extra time on the road.

Route

Namba → Dotonbori → wider inner-city loop → return to base.

What's included

  • Kart rental
  • Helmet and racing suit
  • Insurance
  • Guide
  • Safety briefing

How it works

  1. 01

    Check in

    Arrive at the Namba meeting point, complete paperwork, and get fitted.

  2. 02

    Safety briefing

    A guide walks you through street rules, signals, and the convoy.

  3. 03

    Street ride

    Follow the lead kart through neon streets at a guided pace.

  4. 04

    Return

    Back to base, return the gear, and keep your photos or GoPro clip.
