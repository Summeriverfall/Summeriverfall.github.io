Help

License requirements

You must show a valid license at check-in. Rules vary by country.

  • United States: state driver license + 1949 Geneva IDP recommended.
  • China: Chinese license is not valid on Japanese public roads. Bring a 1949 Geneva IDP with the original license.
  • Japan: a valid Japanese license is accepted.
  • United Kingdom: photocard license. Confirm IDP needs for your issue date.
  • EU/EEA: national license. An IDP can help staff verify details.

Safety notes

Closed-toe shoes, follow the lead kart, no racing, and stop if the guide signals. Helmets stay on while the kart is moving.
