FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions people ask before they ride. License rules differ by country.

License notes by country
Do I need a license?

Yes. Bring a valid license or a 1949 Geneva International Driving Permit. No license, no ride.

Are costumes included?

A racing suit and helmet are included. Themed costumes can be added at booking.

Where do we meet?

Namba area, Osaka. The exact pin is sent after you book.

What if it rains?

Light rain usually runs. Severe weather is rescheduled. We will message you.

How old do I need to be?

Riders must be 18 or older and able to follow the guide on public roads.
